WELCOME TO YOUR GARDEN

A little room to explore.

Each long petal is a slider. Drag its brass handle toward the tip to increase the value. Select a petal to see what it does.

Press the center for a chord, or play the eight small inner petals individually. Your keyboard's A S D F G H J K keys play the same notes.

Turn on Hold the bloom to sustain the center chord, then shape its sound with the petals. Turn it off to release the chord.

Keyboard access: tab to any petal slider and use the arrow keys. Home and End go to the minimum and maximum. Enter or Space plays a focused note.
